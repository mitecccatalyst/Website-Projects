RoutePilot terms of use

Plan smarter, but drive with judgment.

Last updated: May 28, 2026. These terms explain the basic rules for using RoutePilot.

Plain-English Summary

RoutePilot is a planning tool. It can help organize stops, estimate routes, and hand routes to map services. It is not a replacement for safe driving, legal compliance, dispatcher judgment, or official navigation instructions.

Use Of The App

You may use RoutePilot to plan delivery stops, compare route options, organize multi-day trips, export route data, and open map links. You are responsible for checking the final route before driving.

No Guarantee Of Perfect Routes

RoutePilot may show local estimates, exact pins, or live traffic times depending on what services are connected. Road conditions, closures, accidents, weather, legal restrictions, tolls, vehicle limits, parking, loading zones, and business rules can change quickly.

You must confirm routes in Google Maps, Waze, your delivery platform, your dispatch system, or another trusted navigation source before driving.

Third-Party Services

RoutePilot can connect to or open services such as Google Maps, Google Maps Platform, and Waze. Those services have their own terms, pricing, data rules, availability, and limits. RoutePilot is not responsible for third-party outages, route changes, billing, or data handling.

Google API Keys And Backend Use

If you deploy the secure backend, keep the Google Maps API key private. Do not commit API keys to GitHub. Restrict API keys in Google Cloud, monitor usage, and set billing alerts before public use.

Driver Responsibility

  • Follow traffic laws and posted signs.
  • Do not interact with the app while driving unless it is safe and legal.
  • Use hands-free features only where allowed.
  • Do not follow a route into unsafe, illegal, restricted, or unsuitable areas.
  • Check vehicle restrictions, delivery windows, and customer instructions yourself.

Acceptable Use

Do not use RoutePilot to break laws, harass people, collect sensitive personal data without permission, abuse third-party map services, overload backend services, or attempt to extract API keys.

No Warranty

RoutePilot is provided as-is. The app may have bugs, missing data, wrong estimates, or service interruptions. Use it as a helper, not as the final authority for driving decisions.

Updates

These terms may change as RoutePilot adds hosting, collaboration, accounts, paid map services, or other production features.
